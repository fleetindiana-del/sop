1.0
OBJECTIVE                   :
   To lay down the step-wise procedure for Entry and Exit procedure for Visitor Change Room.
2.0
RESPONSIBILITY        :
Visitor, In-charge-Personnel and Administration
3.0
ACCOUNTABILITY     :
QA Head
4.0
PROCEDURE                 :
4.1
Entry Procedure : Annexure-I :-
4.1.1
Open the Door & Enter into the Visitor Change Room.
4.1.2
Take out apron from “Unused Apron” section of cupboard.
4.1.3
Wear the apron.
4.1.4
Wear the cap.
4.1.5
Take out shoe covers & wear the shoe covers taking care that do not touch feet to ground while wearing the shoe covers.
4.1.6
Cross the crossover bench. Take precaution that feet are not touching to the crossover bench.
4.1.7
Sanitize the hands using hand sanitizer.
4.1.8
Open the door and enter into corridor.
4.2
Exit Procedure : Annexure-II :-
4.2.1
Open the Door & Enter into the Visitor Change Room.
4.2.2
Cross the crossover bench. Take precaution that feet are not touching to the crossover bench.
4.2.3
Remove the shoe covers.
4.2.4
Discard the used shoe covers in dustbin.
4.2.5
Remove the cap & apron.
4.2.6
Keep the used apron & cap in the “Used Apron” section of cupboard.
4.2.7
Open the door of visitor change room & exit from the room.
