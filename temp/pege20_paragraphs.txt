RESPONSIBILITY         :
Personnel Head:-
Responsible for planning, scheduling, and conducting annual and need-based safety training sessions, and ensuring overall adherence to workplace safety protocols.
All Employees and Workers:-
Responsible for actively attending required safety training, adhering strictly to safety guidelines and SOPs, wearing assigned Personal Protective Equipment (PPE), and promptly reporting any unsafe conditions or hazards to personnel department.

5.1
The integration of safety awareness and adherence to the safety is mandatory for all personnel to ensure a safe, hygienic work environment and sustain optimal operational productivity.
5.2
The personnel head shall schedule, organize and execute comprehensive safety training for all employees and workers annually, or immediately upon identifying operational needs.
5.3
Training session shall detail all essential safety precautions, emergency protocols and regulatory compliance requirements applicable across factory premises and specialized work zones.
