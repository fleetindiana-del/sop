
1.0`nOBJECTIVE                        :`nTo describe the procedure for cleaning and sanitization of change room.`n`n`n`n2.0`nRESPONSIBILITY             :`nPersonnel and Administration in-charge`n`n`n`n3.0`nACCOUNTABILITY         :`nQA Head`n`n`n`n4.0`nPROCEDURE           :`n`n`n`n`n4.1`nPersonnel and Administration in-charge shall maintain clean and neat change room.`n`n`n4.2`nChange room shall be cleaned two times in a day. Cleaning activity shall be recorded as per annexure-I (Change Room Cleaning Record).`n`n`n4.3`nFloor of the change room shall be cleaned by using 0.1 % Fine Wash solution as cleaning agent. Take 1.0 ml of Fine wash and make volume up to 1.0 ltr. with raw water to prepare 0.1 % fine wash solution.`n`n`n4.4`nDoor of the change room shall be cleaned by using 0.1 % Fine wash solution. Glass shall be cleaned by using liquid glass cleaner such as Colin.`n`n`n4.5`nCross-over bench shall be cleaned by using 0.1 % Fine wash solution. Toilet cleaner such as Harpik shall be used to clean the toilet in the change room. Air purifier like Naphthalene ball shall be used in toilet block for purification of air.`n`n`n4.6`nChange room shall be sanitized by using 2.5 % Dettol / Phenyl solution. Take 25 ml of dettol or phenyl and dilute to 1.0 liter with water for preparing 2.5 % Dettol / Phenyl solution.`n`n`n4.7`nLockers in the change room shall be cleaned daily from outside by using 2.5 % Dettol / Phenyl solution. `n`n`n4.8`nInside of the locker shall be cleaned once at every week. Cleaning activity of lockers shall be recorded as per annexure-II (Lockers Cleaning Record).`n`n
